Research Projects

Exploring AI, Music, and Emotion through Deep Learning and Multimodal Systems

Emotion-Aware Music Generation

Extending Meta’s MusicGen model with an emotion-aware Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) framework that dynamically routes inputs based on emotional context, enhancing expressive music generation.

Latent Emotion Bridging

Developed a dataset of 19,728 emotion-labeled segments from 400 music videos using valence–arousal trajectories. Training multimodal models to learn latent representations bridging emotion cues across audio and video.

Human–LLM Collaboration in Annotation

Investigating alignment and variance between human and LLM-based annotations in musical emotion datasets to evaluate AI feasibility for subjective labeling tasks.
