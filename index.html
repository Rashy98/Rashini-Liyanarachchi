About Me

I am a PhD researcher at the University of New South Wales, focusing on multimodal music emotion recognition using deep learning and natural language processing. My work explores how AI can understand and generate emotionally expressive music, bridging technology and human creativity. Additionally, I investigate the applications of music and AI in therapeutic contexts, studying how emotionally aware AI systems can support music therapy and enhance wellbeing.

Research Interests

  • Music Emotion Recognition (MER)
  • Multimodal Deep Learning
  • AI Creativity and Generative Models
  • Human–AI Collaboration
